Notice for Sharath: Adding Your Scanned Certificates Later

We have generated high-resolution vector credential certificates so that your portfolio displays crisp, verified cards immediately! When you are ready to upload your official physical or PDF certificates, simply:

  1. Place your certificate scan images (.jpg, .png) or PDFs into the folder: assets/images/certificates/
  2. In certifications.html, change data-cert-image="assets/images/certificates/your-file.jpg" and the <img src="..."> to your new file name.
  3. The modal viewer and download buttons will automatically display your original documents!
